WEEVO

Date: Feb 2026  ·  Category: Web Design

WEEVO is an interactive journey through the history of the World Wide Web — from its humble beginnings as a single text page at CERN in 1991 to the intelligent, AI-driven interfaces of today. It traces how the internet evolved across decades, reshaping the way humans communicate, create, and connect.


Whether you're a curious beginner or a tech enthusiast, WEEVO breaks down the major milestones of web evolution Web 1.0, Web 2.0, Web 3.0, and beyond — in a clear and engaging way. Explore how each era transformed not just technology, but culture, business, and everyday life as we know it.
